My Story

The Journey of
Building My Digital Portfolio

📍
Orientation

A few months ago, I realized that as a Software Engineering student, I needed a place to showcase my projects. My name is Wahyu Rizky Setia Ramadhani, a student at SMKN 1 Tengaran. I decided to build my own digital portfolio website from scratch because I wanted it to look professional and represent my identity as a programmer.

⚡
Events

I started by choosing the right tools. I decided to use Tailwind CSS because I wanted a modern and responsive design. At first, I struggled with the layout. I spent a lot of time experimenting with colors until I found the perfect combination: a dark theme with maroon and rose gradients. It looked bold and elegant at the same time.

After the design was ready, I moved on to the features. I added an "About Me" section to tell people that I am from class X PPLG 2. I also created a "Skills" section with animated progress bars for HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

One of the most exciting moments was when I added the "My Services" cards. I applied a floating animation to them. It felt amazing to see my code come to life. However, it wasn't always easy. I faced a bug where my navigation menu wouldn't open on mobile phones. I had to stay up late and carefully check my JavaScript logic until I finally fixed it.

✨
Re-Orientation

After days of hard work, I finally finished the website. I, Wahyu, felt a great sense of accomplishment when I saw my projects, like the furniture workshop and the ticket purchasing design, displayed beautifully on the site. This project taught me that persistence and attention to detail are the most important things in software engineering.
